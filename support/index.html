Quick tips

  • Set your DPI + sensitivity (or cm/360) in the trainer so patterns match your real mouse travel.
  • Use the Zoom slider to magnify the pattern for comfortable tracing.
  • Hold the dot to start — your finger sits just below the aim cursor, so it never hides the ball.
  • Turn on Blind mode to spray from memory, then reveal a per-shot deviation heatmap.
  • Records track your best grade per weapon, per drill, and per mode (Guided / Blind / Moving).
