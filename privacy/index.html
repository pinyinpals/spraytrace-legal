Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Your settings, sensitivity, and training records are stored only on your device. SprayTrace has no user accounts and we do not collect or receive this data.

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's SDK may access your device's advertising identifier — the IDFA on iOS, the Advertising ID on Android — along with your IP address, app interactions and diagnostic information, in line with Google's policies. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SprayTrace has no user accounts, so none of it is tied to an identity we hold.

On iOS, we ask for tracking permission on first launch (Apple's App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are shown non-personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

On Android, there is no equivalent prompt. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, which also lets you opt out of personalized advertising.

In regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent prompt is shown before ads load on both platforms.

Purchases. SprayTrace offers a one-time Pro unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — we never see or store your payment details. The app records only whether the unlock is active on your device.

Google's data practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

SprayTrace Pro (a one-time purchase) removes all ads.

Feedback. If you use Send feedback in the iOS app, your message is sent to us by email through a small relay we run on Cloudflare, delivered by Resend. Along with what you write, it carries the app version, whether Pro is unlocked, your device model and iOS version, and your IP address. The relay keeps no copy; the email is what we hold, and we use it only to read and answer feedback. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

Usage statistics. To see which parts of the app are used and where people stop, SprayTrace (iOS and Android) sends simple app events to our own server: for example that the app was opened, which game, weapon or drill was picked, the grade of a finished run, that the upgrade screen was shown, and whether a purchase, restore or free trial started or finished. Each event carries the app version, your device's OS version, language setting, platform, whether Pro or a trial is active, how many days since you installed, and a random install ID the app creates for itself. That ID is not your advertising identifier (IDFA or Advertising ID), is not linked to your identity, and is never shared with advertisers or used for tracking. Events never contain anything you type. Our server sees your IP address to receive the request but does not store it with the events. Events are deleted after 120 days.

Other than the advertising, usage statistics and feedback form described above, SprayTrace contacts no servers of ours. We do not sell your data.
